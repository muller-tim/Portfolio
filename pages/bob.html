BoB - Battle of Bots

Unity C#

Battle of Bots is a Third-Person-Shooter game where the player has to survive as long as possible against endless enemy waves. The game originated at the GDW Trier Game Jam and is still under development. Players can control their character with keyboard and mouse, using strategic movement and combat to survive increasingly difficult waves.

Future updates include more enemy types, weapon upgrades, and online leaderboards to increase replayability and challenge.
